Book One · The Journey of the Fool's Luck

A strategy role-playing game where magic is sung

Nika and Scott Valorian can hear what the Empire has spent centuries silencing. With the crew of the airship Fool's Luck they run from Emperor Helios's fleet across two realms, under skies where whales fly. Based on the Dual Worlds book saga.

Windows · macOS · Linux

Battles are sung, not swung

Give each fighter their orders, then watch the fight play out: flame serpents, shields of sound, songs that silence a whole line.

The sky is full of whales

Skywhales cross both realms, one for every kind of song. Some watch. Some come down to fight. From your second playthrough on, you can win one for your own company.

And the Empire's fleet

Emperor Helios hunts by air. His dreadnoughts, patrol ships and cage ships stand off over the chapters, and at night their searchlights sweep the streets.

Compose your own magic

Every spell is a song you build in the grimoire: choose its shape, how much voice goes into it, and up to two elements to sing into it. Sing from creation or from negation. Push a singer past their Strain and the power takes over.

A cast of dozens

Singers, drummers, sky captains, librarians, emperors and furies. Everyone has two classes, a trade and their own portrait, and from your second playthrough the whole roster is yours to send anywhere.

And the keepers of the watering holes

Every region has a fishing spot, and every fishing spot has a master with a rod, an opinion and a rumour or two.

Beasts of two natures

Every wild creature in the realms is two animals in one: the Bull-Lion, the Owl-Squirrel, the Shark-Rhino. Hunt them, tame them and stable them, then send them out beside your singers, up to six to a company.

Cook what you catch

Food is half the journey. Every region has a master chef of its own element, and what you hunt, fish and gather on the road goes into the pot. Take a chef's hunts from the notice board to learn their region's cuisine dish by dish, then face the timed hunt into their culinary dungeon to master it.

Weather you fight in

The sky is part of the fight. Weather falls on the board tile by tile, each part of a map can have its own, and it changes what your songs can do: a storm makes Lightning stronger and Fire weaker, pollen makes the wood drowsy, and a burning forest is exactly as bad as it looks.

How it plays

Companies, not single heroes

Move squads across boards in three levels. When two meet, the fight plays out from the orders you gave each fighter.

The sky matters

Storms make Lightning stronger and Fire weaker. Fog, snow, sand, pollen, eclipses and three moons all change a fight.

Beasts and pets

Hunt, tame and stable the realm's creatures, each in elemental forms, and bring them into your companies.

A world between chapters

Travel living maps: dig for relics, fish the watering holes, delve dungeons and visit the circus airship when it moors.

Play it again

Each playthrough adds a layer: new sights, new rules and the whole roster unlocked.
